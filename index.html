01The Chef

A story carried from Bangkok to Leawood

Penny Mufuka came from Thailand in 1994 and worked her way up from busing tables to running her own kitchens. In 2021 she opened Bamboo Penny's in Leawood's Park Place — and Kansas City noticed.

"Kansas City's Best Chef, Penny Mufuka, knows what the people want — and delivers."
— The Pitch

Every dish balances sweet, sour, salty and spice with fresh herbs and open flame — stir-fried, steamed, grilled and slow-stewed the way she learned it.
